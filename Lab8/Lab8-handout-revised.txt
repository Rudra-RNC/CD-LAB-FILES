iii. The nature of semantic analysis indicates the attributes that are to be assigned to grammar symbols,
nonterminals and terminals. These attribute values are assigned, computed and propagated during
parsing. Careful choice of attributes is the first step towards performing of semantic analysis
iv. The calculation of attributes of the LHS nonterminal in terms of the attributes of the grammar symbols
on the RHS is specified as semantic rules or semantic actions and executed when a reduce action is taken
by the parser. This strategy of performing semantic analysis as described above is called as synthesized
attributes based SDTS. In this course, we shall use synthesized attributes only.
v. Synthesized attribute based semantic evaluation is done by embedding the actions within the right hand
side of productions. In the translation scheme
A → X1 X2 . . Xi {action} Xi+1 . . . Xn
action is executed after completion of the parse for Xi. As a special case, we use the following form very
frequently : A → X1 X2 . . Xi Xi+1 . . . Xn {action}, where the action is placed at the end of the rule.
This form permits the attributes of A to be defined as a function of any attributes of the symbols on the
RHS of the rule.

INTERMEDIATE CODE FORM : SYNTAX AND SEMANTICS
In Three Address Code (TAC), each statement contains 3 addresses, two for the operands and one for the result.
The form is similar to assembly code and such statements may have a label, numeric or numeric. We shall use
numeric labels for our experiments. The commonly used three address statements are summarized below.
